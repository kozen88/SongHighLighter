# SongHighlighter: Data Storytelling & Strategic Analytics for Record Labels

A comprehensive, production-grade Visual Analytics and Strategic Consulting ecosystem designed for a modern record label. By analyzing a multi-temporal dataset of Spotify hits (1998–2020) containing over 2,000 distinct tracks, this project maps out the "DNA of hit songs" to answer critical business questions regarding market dominance, audio feature evolution, and emotional mood positioning.

## 🚀 Key Business Dimensions & Strength Points

### 1. Advanced Pre-processing & Feature Engineering
Executed a clean data wrangling pipeline in Python, addressing missing indices, filtering text anomalies, and implementing One-Hot Encoding for categorical variables. Engineered customized metrics to separate long-term historical industry benchmarks from modern streaming audio trends.

### 2. Macro-Scenario Sound Evolution (The Business Funnel)
Applied a *Narrative Funnel* strategy to track the macro-evolution of pop music characteristics over two decades. Designed **Spike Lines** and **Grouped Bar Plots** to compare the acoustic fingerprint of multi-platinum hits against the general market baseline.

### 3. Market Dominance & Competitive Density Analysis
Engineered highly dense interactive visualization layers to isolate market giants from independent labels:
- **StarField Display (Scatter Plot):** Mapping production volume against commercial dominance.
- **Bubble Charts & Gantt Layouts:** Quantifying genre-specific competitive density and analyzing the lifecycle of musical trends over time.

### 4. Emotional Quadrant & Mood Gap Analysis
Implemented Russell's **Circumplex Model of Affect** (Arousal vs. Valence) to map the psychological footprint of tracks. Developed customized Scatter Plots with marginal histograms to identify unexploited "Mood Gaps" in specific musical genres, allowing strategic A&R talent positioning.

### 5. Technical Fingerprinting (Genre Benchmarking)
Built multi-dimensional benchmarking layers using **Parallel Coordinates Plots** and **Radar Charts** to establish the exact audio feature constraints (Loudness, Energy, Danceability) required for a new music product to technically compete with current market leaders.
